DIVISION 01 // PIPELINE

Seven refusal points before a signal exists

The engine does not manufacture calls to stay busy. A signal is the rare residue left when data integrity, macro calendar, higher-timeframe structure, regime, strategy council, calibrated uncertainty, and cost arithmetic all concur.

  1. 01 / CAUSALITY

    Closed-bar boundary

    Drops any bar whose minute has not finished closing. Verified on every push by automated adversarial future-shock tests.

  2. 02 / DATA GATE

    Freshness, continuity, spread

    Rejects stale feeds and excessive spreads. FX models bank-desk session widening; 24/7 spot crypto stays continuous round the clock.

  3. 03 / MACRO BLACKOUT

    Scheduled release shield

    Stands down across US Non-Farm Payrolls (first Friday 08:30 ET, DST-exact) and operator-configured FOMC, CPI, or ECB windows.

  4. 04 / MULTI-TIMEFRAME

    15-minute resampled bias

    Builds closed 5m and 15m structure locally from 1m bars at zero API cost, filtering counter-trend noise on live setups.

  5. 05 / COUNCIL

    Core gate + 12 prototypes

    Core confluence requires 5 of 6 checks including mandatory trend and directional ADX. Prototypes earn weight only after 30 real outcomes.

  6. 06 / META-MODEL & MEMORY

    Calibrated veto & mistake block

    Online logistic ensemble with isotonic calibration plus Wilson-bound mistake memory that blocks regimes where prior calls failed.

DIVISION 05 // HONESTY CHARTER

Six rules enforced by tests, plus one permanent refusal

I

UNRESOLVED stays UNRESOLVED

Missing settlement bars are never silently converted into wins, losses, or ties.

II

Full spread charged on every trade

1.20 pips on EUR/USD, $8.00 on BTC/USD, $0.24 on ETH/USD. Gross-only edges are rejected.

III

10-second seal deadline

Late signals are discarded rather than backdated; late cron wake-ups roll cleanly to the next unopened minute.

IV

SENT requires a Telegram message_id

Transport timeouts become UNRESOLVED alerts, never assumed deliveries.

V

Synthetic data trains nothing

Synthetic series verify plumbing only; weights, calibrators, and promotions require real market bars.

VI

Why 19-of-20 (95%) was refused

A 95% win rate on M1 bars is manufactured only by tiny targets and removing stops — net negative once tail losses hit. Honest net expectancy governs instead.
